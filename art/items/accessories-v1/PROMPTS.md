# Final prompt set

Every asset used this shared direction:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: fantasy RPG inventory sprite for
> Aergyle. Genuinely transparent background; no floor, cast shadow, border,
> frame, vignette, or checkerboard. Wayfarer's Atlas inventory art;
> handcrafted fantasy field specimen, decisive faceted digital paint,
> believable construction, simplified chunky form, crisp silhouette, medium
> stylization, not a photograph or 3D render. Square 1:1 RGBA; one isolated
> subject, optically centered, with ample clear margins and readable at 32px.
> Restrained warm-neutral light from upper left, cool desaturated teal-grey
> self-shadows, and three clear value groups. Humble low-tier equipment; no
> text, lettering, watermark, UI, extra objects, magical effects, glow,
> particles, gems, elaborate filigree, rarity aura, or black background.

The subject-specific portions of the six final prompts were:

## Ironroot Band

One modest hand-forged dark iron ring. The band resembles two gently twisted
root strands with a tiny flattened knot on the front, but remains practical
and wearable. Slight hammer marks, rubbed edges, one small nick, and no
gemstone. Show it in an orthographic-like three-quarter view from slightly
above, filling roughly 76% of the canvas. Use weathered blue-black iron,
subdued brown oxidation, and sparse pale edge wear.

## Tinker's Brass Signet

One plain, slightly chunky brass signet ring used by an itinerant craftsperson.
Its small octagonal face is stamped with a simple shallow four-spoke wheel
mark, not text or a rune. Scuffed shoulders, dulled patina, imperfect handmade
symmetry, and no gemstone. Show the signet face clearly in a three-quarter view
from slightly above, filling roughly 76% of the canvas. Use muted old brass,
umber patina, and restrained pale edge highlights; it must not look shiny or
luxurious.

## Pilgrim's Bronze Ankh

A small weathered bronze ankh pendant on a short loop of coarse dark cord. The
ankh is simple and hand-cast, with slightly uneven arms, softened edges,
green-brown patina in creases, and one repair nick. It is a modest traveler's
devotional token, not a powerful relic. Arrange the complete pendant and cord
in a clean teardrop silhouette with the front face turned slightly toward the
viewer, filling roughly 80% of the canvas. Use dull aged bronze, dark brown
cord, and small areas of muted verdigris.

## Fordstone Talisman

A palm-sized smooth flat river stone, shaped as an irregular rounded triangle,
drilled near the top and hung from a short loop of faded moss-green cord. One
naturally pale mineral vein crosses the grey stone like a ford or winding
stream. The mark is a natural vein, not a glowing rune. Include worn cord
knots and a chipped edge. Show its front face in slight three-quarter view,
filling roughly 80% of the canvas. Use charcoal stone, a pale grey vein, faded
moss cord, and tiny earthen wear.

## Rowan Bead Necklace

A simple necklace of irregular hand-carved rowan-wood beads on dark flax cord.
At its center hangs one small pale bone spacer bead and a short rust-red
tassel. The beads are matte, mismatched, lightly scratched, and plainly made;
there is no pendant, gemstone, or metal. Arrange the full necklace as an open
oval with the central bone bead and tassel at the bottom, shown from slightly
above and filling roughly 82% of the canvas. Use muted reddish-brown wood,
dark flax, aged ivory bone, and dull rust-red fiber.

## Traveler's Backpack

One practical large traveler's backpack made of faded olive-brown canvas and
worn dark leather. It has a bulky rectangular main pack with a flap and two
plain brass buckles, visible shoulder straps, side pockets, a tightly rolled
grey-green wool bedroll across the top, and a coil of simple rope lashed to one
side. It is packed full and noticeably heavy, but believable and mundane, with
a couple of stitched repairs and scuffed corners. Show the entire pack upright
in a three-quarter front view from slightly above, filling roughly 84% of the
canvas. Use coarse canvas, worn leather, wool, and rope with tiny subdued brass
accents. No weapons or potions; the pack must not look luxurious, implausibly
oversized, or magical.

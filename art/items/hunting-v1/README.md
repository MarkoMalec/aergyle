# Hunting material artwork

Mode: `create` (ImageGen), followed by a deterministic 256×256 RGBA runtime
resize. Generated 1254×1254 masters are retained in this directory.

Shared prompt:

> Create one isolated RPG inventory resource sprite for an idle fantasy MMO.
> Use polished hand-painted, lightly faceted fantasy game art; a chunky,
> immediately readable silhouette; believable materials; restrained natural
> colors; warm upper-left light; cool lower-right shading; and a subtle contact
> shadow. Center one fully visible object with generous transparent padding on a
> square canvas, legible at 48px. Use a truly transparent background. No scenery,
> frame, card, pedestal, glow, particles, text, watermark, rarity effects, gore,
> or unrelated props.

Subject prompts:

- Game Meat: two or three tasteful venison-style cuts; no blood or butcher props.
- Raw Hide: one folded and loosely rolled untanned tan-brown hide.
- Small Bones: a compact cluster of four clean warm-ivory animal bones; no skull.
- Sharp Fang: one large curved canine with its natural root.
- Stag Antler: one naturally shed branching red-deer antler.
- Curved Horn: one swept, ridged charcoal mountain-goat horn.
- Animal Sinew: a tidy hank of dried amber tendon fibers with one fiber tie.
- Thick Fur: one folded patch of dense gray-white winter fur; no head or paws.

Runtime exports are in `public/assets/items/resources/hunting/`.

Generated with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool on 16 September 2026. Each
untouched PNG master is stored in its skill's `*-v1` directory. Runtime assets
under `public/assets/skills` are 250px-high panoramic crops encoded as
88-quality WebP files.

All prompts requested premium, grounded fantasy RPG environment art with
realistic materials, simplified readable forms, soft painterly treatment and
important details kept in the central horizontal band for an aggressive 8.7:1
crop. They excluded people, faces, readable writing, letters, numbers, logos,
icons, watermarks, borders and frames.

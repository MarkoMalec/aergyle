## Original prompt direction

A rugged stone foundry with a brick furnace, bellows, soot-dark beams, sparks,
iron tongs, an ingot mold, crucible and newly cast ingots. Controlled orange
furnace light contrasts with charcoal and blue-green shadow. Exclude people,
faces, readable writing, letters, numbers, logos, icons, watermarks, borders and
frames.

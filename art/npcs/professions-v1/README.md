# Profession NPC artwork v1

Generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 19 September 2026.
The untouched 1024×1536 RGBA masters live in this directory. Deterministic
512×768 RGBA runtime exports live under `public/assets/npcs/`.

This first NPC pack follows Aergyle's character art direction: grounded
proportions, a readable three-quarter full-body pose, broad hand-painted planes,
functional clothing, restrained colour, warm upper-left light, cool lower-right
shadow, and a genuinely transparent canvas. Character history is communicated
through wear, repairs, keepsakes, posture, and occupational tools rather than a
painted environment or written labels.

## Ossa — blacksmith

- **Personality:** meticulous, stoic, dependable, and quietly tender.
- **Lifestyle and habits:** starts before dawn, repairs civic ironwork before
  taking decorative commissions, works with one glove and keeps his tools in
  service long after fashionable smiths would replace them.
- **Experience and status:** a veteran master artisan respected by dockworkers
  and city officials; a powerfully built older dwarf, but indifferent to guild
  rank and prestige.
- **History:** he kept a bridge forge operating through a catastrophic flood,
  injured his knee in the chain failure, then left when guild masters took the
  credit.
- **Visible story:** an old burn scar, braced knee, mushroomed hammer, polished
  tong grips, half a discarded bridge-guild seal, indigo apron repairs, one new
  bridge rivet, a child's crude horseshoe charm, weathered ruddy skin, abundant
  shoulder-length iron-grey hair, and a great iron-bound work beard kept clear
  of forge machinery.

## Varka Ten-Reeds — hunter

- **Personality:** patient, skeptical, observant, dryly funny, and fair-minded.
- **Lifestyle and habits:** travels light, repairs every arrow, measures routes
  with knotted cord, and consults a compass whose missing needle she no longer
  needs.
- **Experience and status:** a highly capable frontier guide and careful hunter;
  respected in the Ashwood Verge, economically independent, and still regarded
  with suspicion by some Crown citizens.
- **History:** Crown officials abandoned her patrol during a flood season. She
  filed their crest from her badge and began mapping safe reed paths for anyone
  willing to pay fairly.
- **Visible story:** a neatly bridged split ear, five mismatched repaired arrows,
  the blank ranger badge, missing compass needle, measuring cord, patched bog
  wrappings, a naturally shed antler, and one compact roll of cured hide.

## Pello Nacre — chef

- **Personality:** jovial, inquisitive, hospitable, exacting about seasoning,
  and proud without vanity.
- **Lifestyle and habits:** shops the morning market personally, tastes with the
  same copper spoon, keeps his knives immaculate, and instinctively balances as
  if the floor were still a rolling deck.
- **Experience and status:** a seasoned working chef and beloved market gossip;
  prosperous enough to own good tools and brass fittings, never aristocratic.
- **History:** after a Farwater wreck he cooked the survivors' last shared meal,
  then built a Crownhold soup stall from salvaged galley boards.
- **Visible story:** a distinctive fair, ruddy coastal face with pale blue-grey
  eyes, deep laugh lines and a wind-reddened nose; a comfortable cook's belly;
  a flared eyebrow; salt-marked boots; ship-copper knee brace; cared-for knife
  roll; wooden ship token; market key; repaired apron; Farwater towel; shell
  salt cellar; and a confident skillet of perch and roots.

## Shared constraints

One original character per portrait canvas; complete feet and occupational gear;
no scenery, floor, ground slab, text, frame, watermark, rarity effects, magic,
combat pose, oversized armour, anime, chibi, pixel art, photorealism, glossy 3D,
or painted checkerboard. All pixels outside the character and carried gear are
transparent.

## New profession NPC assets

The seven new characters are intentionally male except **Brogan Leek**, the new
female chef. Their silhouettes avoid the original pack's standard calm
three-quarter stance: Tovin inspects a bent hinge in a heavy, stooped stance;
Sella braces on a walking staff; Brogan holds a dented cooking pot; Halden
carries a hoe across his shoulders; Orrin seals a vial in profile; Marek holds
a closed creel and mended net; and Dain measures a fabric strip with
outstretched arms.

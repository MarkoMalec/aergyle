Generator: built-in `image_gen`. Each character was generated on a 2:3 portrait
canvas. Varka uses her selected first-pass output. Ossa uses two
identity-preserving edits: the first changed the original human woman into a
male dwarf; the second added a light weathered complexion and substantially
more hair and beard. Pello uses a face-focused identity-preserving edit that
gave him a fair Caucasian complexion and slightly fuller belly. Every revision
retained its story props and art direction, and every selected master passed
alpha-channel inspection.

# Elder Angler NPC artwork v1

Seven original male angler NPCs for Aergyle. This pack intentionally limits
the roster to the two requested races—Human and Midget—and every character is
45–100 years old. They are working civilians with age, impairment, weathering,
and specific kit rather than idealized faces, bodies, or heroic-adventurer
silhouettes.

The source masters are 1024×1536 RGBA PNGs in this directory. Runtime exports
are 512×768 RGBA PNGs in `public/assets/npcs/`.

| Name | Race | Age | Character and working kit | Runtime asset |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |

All portraits use the established Aergyle full-body NPC treatment: warm
upper-left light, cool lower-right shading, readable non-combat working poses,
and a dark vignetted alpha treatment consistent with the existing NPC assets.
The accepted prompts and output provenance are in `PROMPTS.md` and
`generation.json`.

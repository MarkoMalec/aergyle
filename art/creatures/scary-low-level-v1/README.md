## Shared generation prompt

> Create one original Aergyle scary low-level common monster on a genuine
> transparent square RGBA canvas. Use polished hand-painted, lightly faceted,
> grounded medieval-fantasy game art; believable construction or anatomy; a
> compact readable silhouette; restrained earthy colors; broad value planes;
> and selective worn texture. Make it eerie and unsettling without graphic
> horror. Show the complete monster centered in a tense alert or stalking
> three-quarter pose, occupying 68–80 percent of the canvas with transparent
> padding and no cropping. Use warm upper-left light and cool desaturated
> lower-right shading. It must read at 48px. Keep the power read clearly
> low-level: small or fragile, crude materials, no ornate armor, giant scale,
> elite weapon, elaborate magic, or boss aura. All pixels outside the monster
> and an optional soft contact shadow must be transparent. No scenery, ground
> slab, frame, text, UI, loot, blood, gore, exposed organs, severed parts,
> excessive rot, bloom, large spell effects, particles, watermark,
> checkerboard, or painted backdrop. Avoid boss designs, graphic body horror,
> cute mascot styling, anime, pixel art, glossy 3D rendering, thick outlines,
> and copied franchise designs.

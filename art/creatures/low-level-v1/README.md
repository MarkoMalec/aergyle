## Shared generation prompt

> Create one original Aergyle low-level common dungeon creature on a genuine
> transparent square RGBA canvas. Use polished hand-painted, lightly faceted,
> grounded medieval-fantasy game art; believable anatomy or material behavior;
> a simple rounded silhouette; restrained natural colors; broad value planes;
> and selective texture. Keep it charming without turning it into mascot art.
> Show the complete creature centered in a calm, alert, or mildly defensive
> three-quarter pose, occupying 66–78 percent of the canvas with generous
> transparent padding and no cropping. Use warm upper-left light and cool
> lower-right shading. It must read at 48px. All pixels outside the creature
> and an optional soft contact shadow must be transparent. Keep it visibly
> low-level: no armor, weapons, battle scars, gore, bared teeth, attack pose,
> frightening expression, scenery, ground slab, frame, text, UI, bloom, large
> magic effects, particles, watermark, checkerboard, or painted backdrop.
> Avoid horror, grotesque details, chibi, plush-toy styling, anime, pixel art,
> glossy 3D rendering, thick outlines, and copied franchise designs.

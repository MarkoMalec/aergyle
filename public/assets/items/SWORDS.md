# Sword Asset Set

All nine icons use the same inventory orientation: handle and pommel at the lower-left, blade tip at the upper-right. They are 256×256 transparent PNGs.

## Restrained-glow swords

Located in `special-mid/weapons/swords/`:

- Ember-Rune Sword — ember and rune glow
- Crystalguard Sword — pale crystal glow
- Moonsteel Sword — cool moonsteel sheen
- Resinbound Sword — warm resin glow

## Standard swords

Located in `weapons/swords/`:

- Militia Sword — broad iron arming sword
- Copper Longsword — copper blade and iron guard
- Iron Falchion — single-edged chopping blade
- Guard Sword — classic steel arming sword
- Steel Bastard Sword — longer two-handed blade
